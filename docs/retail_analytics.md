# Retail Analytics

Retail analytics involves examining sales, revenue, units,
products, categories, locations, channels, pricing and
inventory data to identify patterns and support business
decision-making.

Revenue changes can be investigated across multiple dimensions
such as product category, brand, city and sales channel.

Comparing an unusual period with a recent historical baseline
can help identify which dimensions show the largest changes.

A change observed in a category, brand, city or channel is an
associated signal. It does not by itself establish that the
dimension caused the change in revenue.

Retail analytics should distinguish between observed data,
statistical signals and causal conclusions.
