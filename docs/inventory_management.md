# Retail Inventory Management

Inventory analysis can examine stock on hand, reorder levels,
low-stock records and inventory changes over time.

A low-stock condition occurs when stock on hand is at or below
the relevant reorder level.

Comparing inventory conditions on an unusual sales day with a
recent historical period can help determine whether inventory
conditions changed around the same time.

A reduction in low-stock records does not indicate an inventory
shortage.

Inventory observations should be interpreted together with
sales, pricing and other retail evidence.

Inventory patterns observed around a revenue anomaly are
associated signals and should not automatically be treated as
causes.
